Pioneering Coastal House Construction in Puri

Building a luxury holiday home or permanent residential villa in the sacred coastal city of Puri requires specialized civil engineering. The proximity to the Bay of Bengal exposes structures to constant saline sea breezes, high relative humidity, and severe cyclical monsoons. Standard cement and unprotected rebar will rapidly undergo carbonation, causing internal concrete spalling and early structural decay. As the premier **construction company in Puri**, Aarnikso Construction addresses coastal challenges directly by implementing advanced anti-corrosive treatments and marine-grade materials.

Puri's coastal zoning is strictly monitored. We manage the entire building permit clearance cycle in accordance with the **Puri Konark Development Authority (PKDA)** and **Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ)** parameters. From securing height sanctions in historic sectors near the Shree Jagannath Temple to configuring robust rainwater drainage routes along Puri Bypass Road and Marine Drive, our in-house engineering and planning squads manage the entire structural journey.

The Trusted Building Contractor in Puri

Selecting an experienced, honest **building contractor in Puri** is crucial to prevent premature cosmetic and structural damage.
